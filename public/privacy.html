Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026 · Last Updated: October 2026
✨ SquadIn is a platonic real-world social community: We help people meet in curated small groups at safe, public commercial venues for weekend activities. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

To provide community safety, curated crews, and nearby plan discovery, we collect the following information:

  • Profile Information: Your display name, city, optional bio, weekend interests/passions, and avatar image.
  • Approximate Location: When you grant location permission, we use your device coordinates solely to calculate distances to nearby public plans and users on the Squad Radar (e.g., within 2km, 5km, or 15km). We do not track or store continuous background location.
  • Live Selfie Verification (Optional): If you choose to boost your community trust score, you may take a live selfie. This is used solely to verify genuine community presence and deter fake accounts.
  • In-App Messages & Waves: Chat messages exchanged within unlocked crew plans and lightweight waves sent on the Squad Radar.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Display relevant weekend plans and nearby community members within your chosen city and distance radius.
  • Enable group chat coordination once a weekend crew is accepted by the host.
  • Enforce community safety features, including verified trust badges, Women-Only crew filters, and anti-harassment policies.
  • Automatically prune inactive radar profiles after 7 days of inactivity to keep radar communities fresh and genuine.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. Your data is only shared in the following contexts:

  • Public Profile to Community Members: Other users in your selected city can see your display name, avatar, weekend interests, and approximate distance. Full phone numbers and private credentials are never made public.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: We store application data securely using industry-standard encrypted databases provided by Supabase (PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security) and Vercel hosting over encrypted HTTPS/TLS.

4. Account Deletion & Your Rights

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • One-Tap Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and profile directly from the Profile Settings tab in the app. Upon deletion, your profile record, photos, and active radar listings are immediately removed from our active database.
  • Review & Update: You can edit your name, city, bio, and passions at any time in the app.

5. Children's Privacy

SquadIn is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18.
