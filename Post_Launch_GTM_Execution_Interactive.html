Post-Launch Execution Playbook

SquadIn / WashedUp India — Complete Launch & Growth Strategy

Step-by-step roadmap from Day 0 to Day 90+: How to get users, build liquidity, and turn on monetization.

Phase 1: Day 0 to Day 14 — "The 1-Pincode Launch" (0 to 100 Users)

Hyper-Local Liquidity
Golden Rule: Do not launch across India or even across the whole city. Pick one single neighborhood with high migrant density (e.g., Koramangala/Indiranagar in Bengaluru, Bandra in Mumbai, or Cyber City in Gurgaon).
• Seed the Feed Yourself (Never launch with an empty app):

On Wednesday morning, post 6 curated weekend plans yourself:

Plan 1: Sat 8:00 AM – Cubbon Park Run + Filter Coffee (Cap: 6)
Plan 2: Sat 5:00 PM – Board Games at Third Wave (Cap: 4)
Plan 3: Sun 11:00 AM – Women-only Thrift Store & Boba (Cap: 4)
Plan 4: Sun 4:00 PM – Badminton Doubles at Play Arena (Cap: 4)
• Infiltrate Co-Living Lounges & Notice Boards:

Print 30 simple, aesthetic flyers with a QR code:

"New to the city? Want to grab coffee or play board games this weekend? Scan to join a crew of 4."

Stick them on notice boards inside Zolo, Stanza Living, HelloWorld, Settl, and WeWork cafeterias.

• The Reddit City Subreddit Post:

Post organically on r/bangalore or r/mumbai:

"Hey everyone! We built a simple app for people who don't have friends in the city to do weekend stuff in small groups of 4 (cafes, badminton, concerts). Everything is verified and in public venues. Dropping 5 weekend plans for Koramangala this Saturday—link in comments."
• Attend the First 3 Meetups Personally:

Show up as a participant (without acting like the CEO). Watch the human dynamics, observe where people feel shy, and note what needs fixing.

Phase 2: Day 14 to Day 45 — The Viral Content Loop (100 to 1,000 Users)

Trust & Organic Growth
• Short-Form POV Reels (Organic Instagram & Shorts Growth):

Ask attendees at the end of a fun meetup: "Hey, can I take a 5-second video of all of us saying cheers with our coffees?" Post Reels with captions like:

• "What happens when 4 strangers in Bengaluru meet for filter coffee on Sunday morning ☕"
• "Moved to Bangalore alone vs. Finding my board game crew in 2 weeks ✨"

This format regularly goes viral on Instagram among urban Gen-Z and young techies.

• Recruit 5 Female Community Ambassadors:

Women are the trust anchor of any social platform. If women feel safe, everyone joins. Offer 5 college/corporate ambassadors (e.g. from Swiggy, Flipkart, local design colleges) a "Founding Host" status to post weekly weekend hobby plans.

Phase 3: Day 45 to Day 90 — Turn On Monetization & Venue Deals

Revenue Engine
• Walk into Local Cafes & Turfs on a Tuesday Afternoon:

Meet the cafe manager or badminton court owner:

"I run a social community in Indiranagar. I can bring guaranteed tables of 4–6 paying customers every Saturday afternoon during your slow hours. In exchange, can you give our members 10% off or pay us a ₹50 referral fee per head?"

Cafe and turf owners love guaranteed group footfall.

• Activate Micro-Transactions:

Turn on the Fast-Unlock (₹49) and Bring a Friend (+1 for ₹49) features. You will start seeing daily automated revenue from people unlocking plans early.

Metric Target What It Tells You
1. Fill Rate > 70% Are plans actually filling up and unlocking the chat?
2. Show-Up Rate > 85% Are accepted members actually showing up at the cafe?
3. 30-Day Repeat Rate > 35% Are users coming back to join another plan within 3 weeks?

🚀 Phase 4: Day 90+ — Replicate & Scale

Once your first neighborhood has over 70% fill rate, expand to Neighborhood #2:

Koramangala ➔ HSR Layout ➔ Indiranagar ➔ Whitefield ➔ Mumbai (Bandra) ➔ Pune (Koregaon Park)
