A Real-Life Story: How Rahul, Priya & Ananya Meet in Bengaluru

A true-to-life walkthrough showing how trust, dynamic group sizes, and unlock mechanics feel to users.

🌆 Friday Evening in Koramangala, Bengaluru

Priya (24) moved from Pune to Bengaluru 3 weeks ago for a UI design role. Sitting in her co-living room, she wants to explore local cafes and play board games on Saturday, but her college friends live across the city. Dating apps feel awkward, and large Meetup groups feel overwhelming. Here is how SquadIn solves her weekend in 6 steps:

👩 Priya (24) · User

New city transplant, wants safe weekend friends, loves filter coffee & Catan.

👨 Rahul (26) · Host

Software dev in HSR, regular host, his friends bailed last minute on game night.

👩 Ananya (23) · Plus-One

Priya's roommate, hesitant about meeting strangers until invited as a +1.

1

The 30-Second Trust Verification

Safety Shield

Priya signs up with her phone number and links her corporate email (@swiggy.in). Within 30 seconds, her profile gets a shiny "Pro Verified" badge and a green ID check. She feels relieved seeing that every single profile on the app is verified—zero anonymous trolls or catfish accounts.

2

Rahul Posts the Plan & Smart Auto-Capacity

Dynamic Sizing

Across town, Rahul wants to play board games at Third Wave Coffee, Koramangala on Saturday at 5 PM. He selects the "Cafe / Board Games" category. The app automatically presets the table size to 4 people so nobody has to squeeze into awkward restaurant chairs.

3

The Waiting Room & Anti-Creep Lock

Anti-Spam Lock

Priya browses nearby plans, sees Rahul's board game plan, and taps "Join Spot" along with Vikram (another verified techie). The card now shows 3/4 spots filled. The chat is strictly LOCKED—nobody can spam or slide into private DMs, keeping Priya's inbox 100% clean and safe.

4

The Fast-Unlock & The "+1 Roommate" Expansion

Monetization & Viral Loop

It's Saturday 1 PM (4 hours before the game). With 3/4 filled, Rahul pays ₹49 Fast-Unlock to guarantee the game. Meanwhile, Priya tells her roommate Ananya: "Hey, want to join?" Priya pays ₹49 to add a +1 seat. Ananya quickly verifies her profile, capacity becomes 5/5, and PING! The Group Chat Unlocks for all 5!

5

The Safe, High-Vibe Meetup

IRL Connection

The app drops an automated icebreaker in chat: "Quick check: What is everyone's go-to coffee order?" By 5:00 PM, all 5 members arrive at the cafe. Priya taps the 1-Tap SOS Emergency Share to send the cafe location to her parents. They play Catan, laugh over cold brews, and have an amazing 2 hours.

6

The Trust Karma Loop & Privacy Shield

Reputation

After the game, everyone confirms attendance in the app. Everyone rates the vibe 5/5, boosting their Karma Score to ⭐ 4.9. Priya's ₹49 commitment deposit is instantly refunded to her UPI. In 24 hours, the temporary group chat auto-archives to protect everyone's privacy.

The Result: 5 strangers turned into genuine weekend friends with zero creepiness, zero flakes, and ₹0 awkwardness.
